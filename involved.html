Questions

FAQ

Do I need dance experience to join?
Nope! We welcome everyone. We'll teach you Garba and Raas from the ground up. All you need is enthusiasm.
When are tryouts?
Tryouts are typically held at the beginning of the fall semester. Fill out the interest form to stay in the loop.
How much is the time commitment?
During competition season we practice multiple times a week. Outside of that it varies — we'll always communicate the schedule clearly.
Do I have to be South Asian to join?
Not at all. Tar Heel Raas is open to all UNC students regardless of background. We love sharing our culture with everyone.
