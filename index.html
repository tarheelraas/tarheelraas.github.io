Our Story

More than a dance team

We're a family that works hard, plays hard, and does it together.

Tar Heel Raas was founded in 2006 with a mission to spread our passion for traditional West Indian dance. We love to perform — from competitions around the nation to local events in the heart of Chapel Hill.

We're all-inclusive and no experience is required. Whether you've been doing Garba your whole life or you're picking up the dandiya sticks for the first time, there's a place for you here.

More than anything, Tar Heel Raas is a family. Get ready to feel the THRill.
