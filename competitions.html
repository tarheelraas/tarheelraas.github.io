Competitions & Events

THR competes in Raas All-Stars (RAS), the largest intercollegiate Raas-Garba circuit in the U.S. We travel to competitions hosted at colleges across the country, where teams are judged on choreography, execution, and artistic expression.

Outside of the competition circuit, THR performs at showcases, cultural festivals, and campus events throughout the year.
